# Good-Meals
A small mod that changes how food systems works.

The mod right now is able to hold 3 food items for a different time duration. You can change this in: "WORLD NAME"/serverconfig/goodmeals-server.toml
Said items will give you different small buffs while active and will get you more health. Right now there is a few foods that has regeneration 
and if you have all slots filleds you will get some natural regen boost.

Food is divided in groups: Meat, Fish, Veggies, Fruits, Grain. Eating any food of that group will prevent eating from another of said group.
Cooked food will get better times than raw one, some will get also a better version of said stats (more health, an extra level for effects.)
The mod will prevent you from eating food if you're filled or food is not edible.

Will try to get compat with other foods but it's unlikely for now.
